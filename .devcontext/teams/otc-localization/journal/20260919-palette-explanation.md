## Explanation and next recommendation

- RGB has red, green and blue channels, not separate orange/blue channels. Select ranges in HSV, retaining original RGB values in selected pixels. Deleting green would change orange rather than isolate it.
- Multiplying an already recorded frame by a lower brightness factor does not restore clipped sensor/channel information or increase normalized color separation. For the next recording, expose for the phone screens and lock exposure/white balance after checking both colors. Avoid blanket exposure settings that make distant/blue screens too dim.
- The admin's `CalibrationPanel.tsx:79` currently selects `#FFB000` / `#0066FF` and palette version `amber-blue-v1`; the manifest carries those colors and the client renders them. Masterplan section 5.2 explicitly prefers avoiding saturated red and calls for camera tests; the current palette is a starting choice, not demonstrated optimum. Red/blue needs new physical recordings and coordinated palette-aware worker/client checks. Recoloring this clip cannot simulate a camera observing new emitted colors.
- Recommended subsequent pipeline change: broad palette-supported candidate tracklets during boot, early complete-preamble promotion, then local tracking around verified regions through the packet. Retain original pixels for pilot-relative symbol decisions, small gaps as erasures, and duplicate/crossing rejection. The current worker validates the preamble after scanning; moving that gate earlier is an optimization/improvement still to implement, not claimed done by this diagnostic.
