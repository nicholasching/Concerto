## Load-generator correction

The 8-client public run reached seven eligible clocks in 12.1 seconds, but failed media preload before arming/playback. Railway coalesced the 1 MB response into a large chunk; the generator delayed exposing it for about 31 seconds, creating an artificial inactivity gap beyond the application's 30-second deadline. This is a confirmed pacing defect, not grounds to increase the production timeout. Both public runs stopped and independently returned to 0 connected /stopped /no calibration or pending actions.

The corrected generator splits coalesced HTTP chunks into progressing 32 KiB deliveries and skips empty chunks. Two focused stream tests cover byte preservation/progress and cancellation; fresh sync gate PASS (258 tests). Media reservation now counts each real attempt against the 600 MB ceiling; failed preloads are cancelled during cleanup. Socket close codes, state and clock reply counters are retained on subsequent failures. One local corrected smoke hit a late ACK rejected by the stale-preparation guard; assignment commit now includes the same three-second operator delay as Play to drain duplicate ACKs in this successful-command scenario. Runtime acknowledgement guards remain unchanged. The first public 250-clock stall has not been attributed to the pacing bug; a corrected local 250-client preflight and one bounded public rerun will provide separate evidence.

Corrected **local 250-client preflight PASS** in 113.86 seconds: all 245 modeled eligible phones ready/armed/completed, five deliberate RTT exclusions, zero interrupted patterns/errors, 500 verified transfers /512,022,000 bytes, 30 injected local faults (25 HTTP 503, five idle timeouts), 25 calibration recoveries, five reconnects, 25 playback and 25 assignment recoveries.
