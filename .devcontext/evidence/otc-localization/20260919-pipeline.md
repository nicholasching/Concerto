## Limits and next evidence

The initial 90-second processing target remains unmet, even for these 13.5-second synthetic clips. Real 4K performance with review outputs remains unmeasured. Clean grids do not establish venue recall, exposure/rolling-shutter tolerance, HEVC/HDR support or parallax registration. Dense mapping retains manual anchors where overlap is too narrow; accepted automatic overlap is separately verified with analytic points. No physical rehearsal, browser rendering or hosted CI ran for this branch.
